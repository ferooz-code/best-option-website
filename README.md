# Best Option Cabinet, Counter & Renovation

Website for Best Option, a cabinet, countertop and renovation company at 12372 84 Ave, Surrey, BC.
Office 604-503-4446 · Mobile 778-325-4811

## What is in this repo

- `index.html` is the full site in one file: home, services, bathrooms, materials guide, portfolio, case studies, strata and commercial, about, and the estimate form.
- `img/` holds project photos (WebP) and the vector logo (`mark.svg`, `mark-dark.svg`).
- `robots.txt` and `sitemap.xml` are for search engines. Replace `/` in both with the live domain once you have one.

## Before launch

1. Connect the estimate form and the footer question form to an email service or CRM. Today they show a summary only.
2. Confirm licence, insurance and warranty claims in the trust bar.
3. Put the live domain into `sitemap.xml`, `robots.txt` and the `og:image` tag.
4. Add the website link to the Google Business Profile.

## Hosting

The site is static. Upload the folder to any web host, or turn on GitHub Pages (Settings > Pages > Deploy from branch > main, root).
