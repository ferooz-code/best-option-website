2 cm or 3 cm thickness?

3 cm stone sits directly on cabinets and looks substantial. 2 cm needs a plywood sub-top or a mitred edge for the same look, and costs less per slab. Porcelain comes in 12 mm and 20 mm.

Edge profiles

Eased edges suit modern kitchens and chip least. Mitred edges make an island look like a thick block of stone. Bevel, bullnose and ogee suit traditional rooms.

Seams and vein matching

Slabs run about 3 by 1.6 metres. Longer runs need a seam. Ask where the seams go before cutting, and book-match veined slabs so the pattern flows across.

Sinks and cut-outs

Undermount sinks need stone, quartz, porcelain or solid surface. Cooktop, faucet and soap cut-outs are priced per hole, so list them before quoting.

Hardware matters

Part 3 · Design

What makes a kitchen beautiful

Ten rules our designers follow. The numbers come from standard kitchen planning guidelines.

  1. Rule 1

    Plan the work zones first

    Sink, cooktop and fridge sit within a few steps of each other. Keep each leg between 1.2 and 2.7 metres.

  2. Rule 2

    Give aisles room

    Leave 42 inches between counters for one cook and 48 inches for two. Leave 36 inches behind island seating.

  3. Rule 3

    Size the island overhang

    Allow 15 inches of knee space at 36-inch counter height and 12 inches at 42-inch bar height. Allow 24 inches of width per stool.

  4. Rule 4

    Run uppers to the ceiling

    Ceiling-height cabinets add storage, remove the dust shelf on top and make the room look taller.

  5. Rule 5

    Layer the lighting

    Pot lights for the room, LED strips under the uppers for tasks, and pendants over the island for character.

  6. Rule 6

    Run the stone up the wall

    A full-height slab backsplash in the counter material has no grout lines and makes the stone the feature.

  7. Rule 7

    Limit finishes to three

    One cabinet colour, one stone, one metal. Add a second cabinet colour on the island only.

  8. Rule 8

    Hide the clutter

    Panel the dishwasher, build an appliance garage, and give the microwave its own cabinet or drawer.

  9. Rule 9

    Choose drawers over doors

    Deep drawers in base cabinets put pots and dishes in reach without kneeling.

  10. Rule 10

    Spend on what you touch

    Counters, drawer slides, faucet and pulls take daily wear. Save on interior shelving and secondary rooms.

Part 4 · Bathrooms and washrooms

Bathroom materials that last

Waterproof before you tile

Tile is not waterproof. A membrane behind shower walls and under the floor protects the framing. Ask your contractor which system they use.

Vanity tops

Quartz is the default for vanities: no sealing, no etching from toothpaste or cleaners. Porcelain suits thin, floating vanities.

Large-format tile

Fewer grout lines mean less cleaning. Large porcelain tile needs a flat floor, so budget for levelling.

Ventilation

A quiet fan vented outside, run 20 minutes after a shower, protects paint, grout and cabinets from moisture.

FAQ

Common questions

How much does a kitchen renovation cost in Surrey, BC?

Most full kitchen renovations in the Lower Mainland fall between about $40,000 and $110,000 before GST. Size, cabinet construction, countertop material and moving walls, plumbing or electrical change the number. Use the budget calculator for a range, then book a free line-item estimate.

Is quartz or granite better for kitchen countertops?

Quartz is non-porous, needs no sealing and gives a consistent colour. Granite handles heat better and every slab is unique, but needs sealing every one to two years. Both suit busy kitchens.

How long does countertop installation take?

After templating, fabrication and install usually take days, not weeks, depending on slab stock and cut-outs. Call 604-503-4446 for the current lead time.

Do you renovate bathrooms and commercial washrooms?

Yes. We renovate home bathrooms and commercial washrooms: vanities, tile, waterproofing, fixtures, plumbing, lighting and accessible layouts.

Do I need a permit?

Moving walls, plumbing, gas or electrical usually needs a City of Surrey or local municipal permit. Cabinet and countertop swaps usually do not. We confirm during the site visit and handle the application.

Which areas do you serve?

Surrey, Delta, Langley, White Rock, Burnaby, Richmond, New Westminster, Coquitlam, Vancouver and the rest of the Lower Mainland.
